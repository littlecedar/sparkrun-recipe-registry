## How to read 'verdict' and 'McNemar p'

`discordant` counts the items the two models disagreed on. If a gap were real, those disagreements would be lopsided; `McNemar p` is the exact two-sided probability of the observed (or more lopsided) split under a fair coin. `p >= 0.05` → **tie** (the gap is consistent with noise). Small `n` and near-ceiling benchmarks (ARC, HumanEval, MATH-500) have very few discordant items, so only a large, consistent gap could ever reach significance here.
